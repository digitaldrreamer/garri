Quarterly Statement

This document exists to be fragmented. It is long enough to span several pages and carries the constructs that make page fragmentation more than arithmetic: blocks that refuse to split, headings that must travel with the text beneath them, a table whose rows cross a page boundary, an explicit forced break, and a paragraph with widow and orphan constraints.

The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. Each of these paragraphs is padded to a length that guarantees wrapping at the measured column width, so that individual line boxes accumulate and eventually collide with the bottom edge of a page. Line boxes are the unit that actually matters here, because a page break falls between lines and never through them.

Where a break falls is decided by more than the running height. A block with break-inside avoid will be pushed wholesale to the next page rather than divided. A heading with break-after avoid will follow its paragraph. Orphan and widow counts pull additional lines across the boundary. None of these are visible in a continuous screen layout, which is precisely the question under test.
